Privacy Policy

RideLocker: Vehicle Reminders (package com.ridelocker.app) for Android.

Effective 8 October 2026 · Last updated 8 October 2026

The short version

  • Your vehicle details, documents and photos are stored only on your phone.
  • RideLocker has no account, no sign-in and no servers. We never receive your data.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and we never sell or share your information.
  • Documents are read on your device, not in the cloud.
  • The app connects to the internet only to check for app updates, to download the optional offline AI model, and for Google Play services. It sends none of your records.

1. Who we are

RideLocker is developed and published by Vivek Tarnallya, an individual developer based in India ("we", "us"). For any privacy question, email vivektarnallya@gmail.com.

2. Information you keep in the app

You may add the following to RideLocker:

  • Vehicle details, such as registration number, make, model and fuel type.
  • Document details, such as policy, licence or certificate numbers, owner name, and issue and expiry dates (RC, insurance, PUC, driving licence, fitness, permit and similar papers).
  • Photos, scans and PDF files of those documents.
  • Reminders, notes and app preferences.

All of this is saved in the app's private storage on your phone. We do not collect it, cannot see it, and have no copy of it. It leaves your phone only if you choose to back it up, export it or share it, as described below.

3. How documents are read

When you scan or import a document, RideLocker reads its text on your phone using Google's ML Kit text recognition, which runs on-device. Your document images are not uploaded for this. Google may collect limited, anonymous diagnostic and usage information about ML Kit's performance, as described in Google's ML Kit terms.

Ride AI (optional)

Ride AI is an optional assistant that runs entirely on your phone. If you turn it on, the app downloads a language model file (about 1.1 GB) from Hugging Face, a public model host. That download is an ordinary file request. Hugging Face can see your IP address and device details, as any website can, but it receives none of your records or questions. After the download, Ride AI works offline, and your questions and answers never leave your phone.

4. Permissions we ask for

CameraTo photograph your documents. Photos are saved inside the app only.
Photos and filesThrough Android's own picker, to import an image or PDF you select, or to choose a backup folder. The app can only open the files and folders you pick.
Notifications, exact alarms, run at startupTo show renewal reminders on time, and to reschedule them after your phone restarts. Reminders are created on your phone, not sent from a server.
Fingerprint / face unlockFor the optional app lock. Android handles your biometrics; RideLocker never receives them.
InternetOnly for the connections listed in section 5.

RideLocker does not request your location, contacts, microphone, call logs or SMS.

5. Internet connections the app makes

  • App updates (Expo). When the app opens, it checks with Expo's update service (expo.dev) for an improved version of the app's code. The request includes technical details only: app version, platform and update channel. As with any connection, Expo can see your IP address. See Expo's privacy policy.
  • Offline AI model download (Hugging Face). Only if you turn on Ride AI, as described in section 3.
  • Google Play. Google Play installs and updates the app, handles your purchase, and may check that your copy is genuine. See Google's privacy policy.

None of these connections carries your vehicle details, documents, photos, reminders or notes. RideLocker contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

6. Backups

  • Backup folder you choose. If you turn on automatic backup, RideLocker copies your records and document images into a folder you select. If that folder is synced to a cloud service (for example Google Drive), that provider's privacy policy applies to the copy.
  • Android backup. If backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may save RideLocker's records database (not the document images) to your Google account. Google stores and protects this backup, and we cannot access it. You can turn it off in your phone's backup settings.
  • Phone-to-phone transfer. When you move to a new phone with Android's transfer tool, your records and document images may be copied directly to the new device.

7. Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent, trade or share your information with anyone, because we never receive it. If you use the app's share or export features (for example, to send a document or a PDF), it goes only to the app or person you choose.

8. Purchases

RideLocker is sold through Google Play. Google processes your payment. We receive standard purchase records from Google (such as an order number, date, country and price) and never see your card or bank details.

9. Security

Your data is protected by Android's app sandbox, so other apps cannot read it, and you can add a fingerprint or face lock inside RideLocker. The app also checks that it is a genuine copy and refuses to run if it has been modified. Your phone's own screen lock is an important part of keeping your papers safe, so we recommend using one.

10. Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it. You can:

  • delete any vehicle, document or reminder inside the app;
  • uninstall RideLocker, which removes all of its data from your phone;
  • delete backup folders yourself, and manage Android backups in your Google account settings.

There is no account to close and nothing stored on our side to delete.

11. Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and similar laws, you have rights to access, correct and erase your personal data. Because your data is held only on your device, you can exercise these rights directly in the app at any time. For anything else, contact us at the email above and we will reply within 30 days.

12. Children

RideLocker is meant for vehicle owners and drivers, and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change how RideLocker handles data, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
